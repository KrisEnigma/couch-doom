CouchDoom - play your DoomRunner presets from the couch with a gamepad
https://github.com/KrisEnigma/couch-doom

1. Set up your presets in DoomRunner first (https://github.com/Youda008/DoomRunner).
2. Double-click CouchDoom.exe. That's it.

CouchDoom finds DoomRunner's settings on its own. If you use the portable DoomRunner,
you can also put this CouchDoom folder inside the DoomRunner folder.
If it can't find them, it tells you where it looked.

Controls (Xbox names; PlayStation and Switch pads work too)
  A / Start   Play          B   Quit (press twice) or close
  X           Info sheet    Y   Search
  LB / RB     Jump section  R3  Add to favorites
  Keyboard: arrows, Enter to play, Esc to quit, or just type to search.
  Mouse: click a preset to select it, click again to play. Wheel scrolls,
  right-click goes back, and the button hints at the bottom are clickable.

Windows may warn that the app is from an unknown publisher (it isn't code-signed).
Click "More info" then "Run anyway".

MIDI title music needs a SoundFont: drop any .sf2 file into a "soundfonts" folder next
to CouchDoom.exe. Without one, MIDI music stays silent and everything else works.
