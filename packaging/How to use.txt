CouchDoom
Play your Doom launcher setups with a gamepad, on the TV or a Steam Deck.
Made by KrisEnigma: https://github.com/KrisEnigma/couch-doom

GETTING STARTED

1. Set up games in DoomRunner, ZDL or Doom Launcher. You can also use GZDoom,
   UZDoom or VKDoom by itself; CouchDoom lists the IWADs it finds.
2. Start CouchDoom:
   Windows: Run CouchDoom.exe.
   macOS: Open the DMG and drag CouchDoom.app to Applications, then open it there.
   Linux: Run ./CouchDoom.

STEAM DECK

Use the Linux x64 download. In Desktop Mode, extract it, then in Steam choose
"Add a Non-Steam Game" and browse to CouchDoom. It then starts from Game Mode like
any other game. GZDoom and DoomRunner from Discover are detected automatically.
Library art: https://github.com/KrisEnigma/couch-doom/tree/main/packaging/steam

FINDING YOUR GAMES

CouchDoom finds launcher settings automatically, including portable installs with
a Start Menu, Desktop or taskbar shortcut. You can also put the CouchDoom folder
inside a portable launcher's folder.

If it finds more than one launcher, it asks which one to use and remembers the answer.
Press L3 (click the left stick) or F4 any time to switch.

If it finds none, press L3 / F4 and choose "Find it myself..." to browse to the
launcher's or port's program, or drag the program (or its folder) onto the CouchDoom window.
In the browser use the D-pad/stick to move, A to open or choose, B to go up, X to show
all files, LB/RB to switch roots, and LT/RT to page.

CONTROLS
Xbox button names are used below. PlayStation and Switch controllers work too.

  A / Start   Play          B   Quit (press twice) or close
  X           Info sheet    Y   Search
  LB / RB     Jump section  R3  Add to favorites
  L3          Switch launcher
  View/Back   Music on or off
  LT / RT     Page up / down; in the info sheet the right stick scrolls too
  Keyboard: arrows, Enter to play, Esc to quit, Tab for info, F2 music,
  F3 favorite, F4 switch launcher, Page Up / Down, or just type to search.
  Mouse: click a preset to select it, click again to play. Wheel scrolls,
  right-click goes back, and the button hints at the bottom are clickable.

FIRST LAUNCH

CouchDoom isn't code-signed, so your system may ask you to approve the first launch.
Windows: On the "unknown publisher" warning, click "More info" then "Run anyway".
macOS: If the app can't be opened or verified, open System Settings > Privacy &
  Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to CouchDoom.

MIDI MUSIC

MIDI title music uses the selected source port's configured or included SoundFont
when available. Chocolate Doom and its forks can use the FluidSynth SoundFont from
their settings. CouchDoom includes a default SoundFont for ports without one.
Use --soundfont <file> to choose another. A SoundFont in a "soundfonts" folder beside
CouchDoom.exe, CouchDoom.app or CouchDoom is used when the port has no own bank.

MUSIC CACHE

Non-MIDI title music is cached so it can stream without keeping the whole track in
memory. The cache is separate from title images and other menu assets:
  macOS: ~/Library/Caches/CouchDoom/music
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\CouchDoom\cache\music
  Linux: $XDG_CACHE_HOME/couch-doom/music (or ~/.cache/couch-doom/music)
Close CouchDoom before clearing this folder; the cache is recreated as tracks are played.

TROUBLESHOOTING AND LOGS

CouchDoom logs game launches, SoundFont searches and MIDI music status to:
  macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/CouchDoom/state/couch-doom.log
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\CouchDoom\state\couch-doom.log
  Linux: ~/.local/share/couch-doom/state/couch-doom.log
You can also launch CouchDoom from a terminal to see music and engine logs in real time.
