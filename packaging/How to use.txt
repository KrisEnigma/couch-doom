CouchDoom - play your Doom launcher's setups with a gamepad, on the TV or a Steam Deck
Made by KrisEnigma - https://github.com/KrisEnigma/couch-doom

1. Set up your games in DoomRunner, ZDL or Doom Launcher first. No launcher? GZDoom,
   UZDoom or VKDoom on its own works too: CouchDoom lists the IWADs it can find.
2. Start it: CouchDoom.exe on Windows, CouchDoom.app on macOS, ./CouchDoom on Linux.

Steam Deck: use the Linux x64 download. In Desktop Mode, extract it, then in Steam
choose "Add a Non-Steam Game" and browse to CouchDoom. It then starts from Game Mode
like any other game. GZDoom and DoomRunner from Discover are found automatically.
Library art for the shortcut: github.com/KrisEnigma/couch-doom/tree/main/packaging/steam

CouchDoom finds your launcher's settings on its own, including portable installs you
have a Start Menu, Desktop or taskbar shortcut to. You can also put this CouchDoom
folder inside a portable launcher's folder.

If it finds more than one launcher, it asks which one to use and remembers the answer.
Press L3 (click the left stick) or F4 any time to switch.

If it finds none, press L3 / F4 and choose "Find it myself..." to browse to the
launcher's or port's .exe, or drag the .exe (or its folder) onto the CouchDoom window.

Controls (Xbox names; PlayStation and Switch pads work too)
  A / Start   Play          B   Quit (press twice) or close
  X           Info sheet    Y   Search
  LB / RB     Jump section  R3  Add to favorites
  L3          Switch launcher
  View/Back   Music on or off
  LT / RT     Page up / down; in the info sheet the right stick scrolls too
  Keyboard: arrows, Enter to play, Esc to quit, Tab for info, F2 music,
  F3 favorite, F4 switch launcher, Page Up / Down, or just type to search.
  Mouse: click a preset to select it, click again to play. Wheel scrolls,
  right-click goes back, and the button hints at the bottom are clickable.

CouchDoom isn't code-signed, so the first start needs one extra step:
  Windows: on the "unknown publisher" warning, click "More info" then "Run anyway".
  macOS: if it says the app can't be opened or verified, open System Settings >
  Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to CouchDoom.

MIDI title music needs a SoundFont. CouchDoom uses the ones that come with GZDoom,
UZDoom or VKDoom, so usually there's nothing to do. To use a different one, drop any
.sf2 file into a "soundfonts" folder next to CouchDoom.exe, CouchDoom.app or CouchDoom;
the largest file there wins. Without any, MIDI music stays silent and everything else
works.
