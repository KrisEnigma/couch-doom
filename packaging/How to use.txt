CouchDoom
=========

Play your Doom launcher setups with a gamepad, on the TV or a Steam Deck.
Made by KrisEnigma: https://github.com/KrisEnigma/couch-doom

GETTING STARTED
---------------

1. Set up games in DoomRunner, ZDL, Doom Launcher or Doom Launcher 667. You can
   also use GZDoom, UZDoom or VKDoom by itself; CouchDoom lists the IWADs it
   finds.
2. Start CouchDoom:
   Windows: Run CouchDoom.exe.
   macOS: Open the DMG, drag CouchDoom.app to Applications, then open it there.
   Linux: Run ./CouchDoom.

STEAM DECK
----------

Use the Linux x64 download. In Desktop Mode, extract it, then in Steam choose
"Add a Non-Steam Game" and browse to CouchDoom. It then starts from Game Mode
like any other game. GZDoom and DoomRunner from Discover are detected
automatically.

Library art:
https://github.com/KrisEnigma/couch-doom/tree/main/packaging/steam

FINDING YOUR GAMES
------------------

CouchDoom finds launcher settings automatically, including portable installs
with a Start Menu, Desktop or taskbar shortcut. You can also put the CouchDoom
folder inside a portable launcher's folder. It never changes your launcher
setups.

If it finds more than one launcher, it asks which one to use and remembers your
answer. Open the menu (Start on a controller, Esc on a keyboard) and choose
"Choose launcher" any time to switch.

If it finds none, open the menu, choose "Choose launcher", then "Find it
myself..." to browse to the launcher's or port's program. You can also drag the
program or its folder onto the CouchDoom window.

In the browser, use the D-pad or stick to move, the confirm button to open or
choose, and the back button to go up. Use the show-all-files control to reveal
hidden items. Select "Parent folder" to go up; at a drive root, select
"Drives & home" for other locations. The triggers page through long lists. An
installed copy starts in CouchDoom's own folder.

CONTROLS
--------

The app shows button prompts for your controller. These are the names used on
Xbox, PlayStation and Switch controllers.

Play or choose: A (Xbox/Switch); Cross (PlayStation)
Back or close a panel: B (Xbox/Switch); Circle (PlayStation)
Info sheet (on a group heading, expand or collapse every group): X (Xbox/Switch);
Square (PlayStation)
Search: Y (Xbox/Switch); Triangle (PlayStation)
Open the menu: Start (Xbox), Options (PlayStation), + (Switch)
Quick help: View (Xbox), Create (PlayStation), - (Switch)
Previous / next section: LB/RB (Xbox), L1/R1 (PlayStation), L/R (Switch)
Add or remove favorite: R3 (press the right stick)
Page up / down: LT/RT (Xbox), L2/R2 (PlayStation), ZL/ZR (Switch)

Keyboard: arrows to move, Enter to play, Esc for the menu, Tab for info, F1 for
help, F3 for favorite, F5 for Help & Feedback, Page Up and Page Down to page,
Home and End for the ends of the list or browser, or type to search.

Mouse: click once to select a preset and again to play. The wheel scrolls,
right-click goes back, and the button hints at the bottom are clickable.

MENU
----

Start or Esc opens the menu: return to the list, refresh launcher data, choose a
launcher, toggle title music or menu sounds, cycle Colors and Language, turn the
optional Recently played group on or off, open Help, or quit.

Language follows your system on first run when CouchDoom supports it (English,
Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German). Colors can follow each game's
art or lock one look for the whole app.

FIRST LAUNCH
------------

CouchDoom isn't code-signed, so your system may ask you to approve the first
launch.

Windows: On the "unknown publisher" warning, click "More info" then
"Run anyway".

macOS: If the app can't be opened or verified, open System Settings, then
Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to CouchDoom.

MIDI MUSIC
----------

Title music that is MIDI plays automatically. CouchDoom ships with a SoundFont,
and prefers the selected source port's own bank when it has one (including the
FluidSynth SoundFont Chocolate Doom and its forks can set in their settings).

To use a different bank, pass --soundfont <file>, or put a SoundFont in a
"soundfonts" folder beside CouchDoom.exe, CouchDoom.app or CouchDoom.

MUSIC CACHE
-----------

Non-MIDI title music is cached so it can stream without keeping the whole track
in memory. The cache is separate from title images and other menu assets:

macOS:  ~/Library/Caches/CouchDoom/music
Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\CouchDoom\cache\music
Linux:  $XDG_CACHE_HOME/couch-doom/music (or ~/.cache/couch-doom/music)

Close CouchDoom before clearing this folder; the cache is recreated as tracks
are played.

UPDATES
-------

Packaged builds check GitHub once after startup and can install an update from
inside the app, then restart. Source builds open the release page instead.

TROUBLESHOOTING AND LOGS
------------------------

CouchDoom logs game launches, SoundFont searches and MIDI music status to:

macOS:  ~/Library/Application Support/CouchDoom/state/couch-doom.log
Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\CouchDoom\state\couch-doom.log
Linux:  ~/.local/share/couch-doom/state/couch-doom.log

You can also launch CouchDoom from a terminal to see music and engine logs in
real time.
